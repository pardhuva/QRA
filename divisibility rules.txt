
//divisibility rules 

div by 1 -> every number is divisible by 1 
div by 2 -> last digit is even
div by 3 -> sum of the digits is multiple of  3 
div by 4 -> last two digits are multiple of  4 
div by 5 -> last digit 0 or 5 
div by 6 -> div by 2 and 3 
div by 7 -> 
            1. double last digit 
            2. subtract the remaining digits 
            3. result must be multiple of 7 
  ex: 294   -> 4*2 = 8 
               29-8 = 21%7 =0 so it is div of 7
div by 8 -> last 3 digits is multiple of 8 
div by 9 -> continue adding the digits until it reaches 9 
    ex: 8766 
        1. 8+7+6+6 = 27 
        2. 2+7 = 9 (so div by 9)
div by 10 -> last digit must be 0 
div by 11 -> subtract sum of even numbered places from sum of odd numbered places 
             difference must be a multiple of 11 
div by 12 -> div by 3 and 4 
div by 13 -> multiply last digit by 4 adn add remaining digits result must be multiple of 13 

div by x then it should be div by its coprimes 
  coprimes -> whose hcf is 1 
ex: div by 12 -> 3 adn 4 
    div by 14 -> 2 and 7
    div by 15 -> 3 and 5
